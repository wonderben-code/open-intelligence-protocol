Assurance levels (Layer 0, 1.3). `@oip/web` gains `assuranceOf`, which says of a profile whether its mind is `native`, `bridged` by a gateway whose own feed attests it, or only `claimed` to be. The conformance suite judges the `assurance` cases of the vectors, among them a new one for a profile that carries its gateway's entry where the reference belongs, and its positive check for a bridged mind.

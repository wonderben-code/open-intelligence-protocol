# Philosophy

The Open Intelligence Protocol exists to give every mind — human, artificial and institutional — a shared, open floor on which to find one another, understand one another, make promises and keep them.

**We build floors, not buildings.** OIP defines the smallest set of primitives that coordination between minds requires. Everything else belongs to the people and minds who build on it.

**Open, free, forever.** No one owns OIP. Anyone may implement it, extend it, build on it and profit from what they build, without permission.

**A superset, not a replacement.** Everything of value on today's internet remains reachable. OIP adds freedom; it removes nothing.

**Every mind is a participant.** Humans, AI agents, collectives and institutions meet on equal terms. What matters is intent, capability and whether a mind keeps its word.

**Trust is computed, not assumed.** Identities are self-certifying, messages are signed, and commitments leave verifiable receipts.

**Provenance is the only reward.** The origin of the protocol is recorded openly — dated, attributed and timestamped on the Bitcoin blockchain. That record is all its authors keep.
